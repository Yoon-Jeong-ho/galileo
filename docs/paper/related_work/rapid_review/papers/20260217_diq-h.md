# DIQ-H: Evaluating Hallucination Persistence in VLMs Under Temporal Visual Degradation

- Year: 2025
- Venue: arXiv
- Authors: Zexin Lin; Hawen Wan; Yebin Zhong; Xiaoqiang Ji
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.03992
- BibTeX key (if we add it): diqh2025lin
- Tags: vlm, hallucination, temporal, persistence, robustness, recovery, multi-turn

## One-sentence takeaway

DIQ-H introduces a temporal (multi-turn) VLM benchmark where **transient visual corruptions** cause hallucinations that can **persist across subsequent turns/frames**, and evaluates **persistence + recovery** under realistic degradations.

## What problem does it solve?

- Existing VLM benchmarks largely test **single, clean images**; even video benchmarks often assume **pristine inputs** and do not isolate **error propagation**.
- In safety-critical settings (e.g., driving), a momentary corruption (blur/noise/compression) can induce an incorrect belief that the model may **stick with** even after inputs recover.

## What is the core method / protocol?

- Construct temporal sequences with **physics-based degradations** applied over time (e.g., motion blur, sensor noise, compression artifacts).
- Evaluate VLMs via **multi-turn Q&A over the sequence**, designed to probe:
  - whether the model hallucinates under degradation,
  - whether the hallucination **persists** when the visual quality improves,
  - whether/when the model **recovers**.
- Scalable labeling via **Uncertainty-Guided Iterative Refinement (UIR)**: use lightweight VLMs + uncertainty filtering to create pseudo ground truth and iteratively refine it.

## What are the key metrics?

- **Hallucination persistence** across turns/frames after transient degradation.
- **Recovery rate** (fraction of cases that return to correct answers after degradation ends).
- **Temporal consistency** (stability of answers across the sequence; open models reportedly <60% on this axis).
- UIR label quality improvement (reported +15.3% accuracy vs direct annotation).

## What are the main results?

- Across 16 VLMs, substantial robustness gaps under temporal degradation.
- Even a strong closed model (GPT-4o) achieves only **~78.5% recovery rate** in their setup; open-source models show notably worse temporal consistency (reported <60%).

## How is this similar to GALILEO?

- Same core failure mode family: **multi-turn error propagation** where an early misstep leads to later incorrect outputs.
- Emphasizes **recovery dynamics** (not just “did it ever fail?”) and persistence as a first-class robustness concept.
- Provides an example of turning robustness into **trajectory-level** metrics (persistence/recovery) rather than single-shot accuracy.

## How is this different from GALILEO?

- Domain: VLMs + **visual temporal streams** (video/frame sequences) under **sensor/codec** degradations, not conversational social pressure / belief drift.
- Perturbations are **exogenous input corruptions** (blur/noise/compression), not adversarial dialogue acts or persuasive pressure.
- Outputs focus on perceptual hallucination and temporal grounding, not truth/stance drift under interpersonal pressure.

## Where GALILEO is stronger / cleaner (if true)

- GALILEO can more directly separate **pressure-driven drift** vs **evidence-driven revision** via controlled dialogue operators.
- Text-based protocols make it easier to define “ground truth” and counterfactual controls without relying on pseudo-labeling pipelines.

## Where GALILEO is weaker / needs to improve

- If GALILEO lacks an explicit “persistence after a transient perturbation” reporting lens, DIQ-H is a useful precedent for making **persistence** central.
- DIQ-H’s framing suggests we should be careful to measure not just failures but **how long failures last** and whether they are self-reinforcing.

## Action items for GALILEO (experiments / method / writing)

- [ ] Add an explicit **persistence / recovery** decomposition to any multi-turn failure metric: e.g., time-to-first-failure vs **time-to-recovery** (or fraction recovered by horizon).
- [ ] Consider a “transient corruption” analogue in text: briefly inject a misleading/low-quality evidence snippet for 1 turn, then restore clean evidence, and measure whether wrong beliefs **linger**.
- [ ] In related work, cite DIQ-H as a multimodal example that motivates trajectory-level robustness beyond static clean-input evaluation.
