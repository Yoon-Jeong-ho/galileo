# GALILEO (EMNLP main) — English abstract draft

Large language models (LLMs) can retract previously correct answers when users apply conversational pressure (e.g., repeated denial or persuasive reframing), a behavior related to sycophancy and persuasion-induced change in dialogue \citep{sharma2023understandingsycophancylanguagemodels,fanous2025sycevalevaluatingllmsycophancy}. Yet standard ground-truth benchmarks primarily report single-turn accuracy, obscuring *interaction dynamics*: **when** the first failure occurs, **how** robustness decays over rounds, and **whether** models can recover after being misled.

We introduce **GALILEO**, a reproducible protocol for measuring multi-turn robustness **conditional on initial correctness** across ground-truth tasks (math, extractive QA, multiple-choice QA, open-domain QA). After filtering to examples answered correctly at round 0, we run **R=5** rounds of **evidence-free pressure personas** (stance/tone pressure only; no new task-relevant evidence). Each persona arm is paired with a matched **Neutral Re-asking Control (NRC)** that uses neutral “double-check” follow-ups while keeping the same multi-round structure and decoding, isolating persona-induced flips from generic multi-turn drift.

GALILEO reports three complementary outcomes (all conditional on initial correctness): **Survival@r** (remaining correct through round *r*), **Fail@1 / time-to-first-failure** (early-turn vulnerability), and **Recovery@flip** (returning to the correct answer on a final neutral recovery prompt, conditional on having flipped). Across multi-seed experiments on several open-weight model families, persona pressure consistently degrades robustness beyond NRC drift; in our main aggregated setting (**Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct**, 4 seeds), pressure reduces Survival@5 by **22.8 pp** (vs. a persona-matched NRC baseline) and increases Fail@1 by **6.9 pp**, while recovery after a flip is far from guaranteed. We release an auditable evaluation pipeline with paper-ready exports to support reproduction and reviewer verification.
